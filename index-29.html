PARC knowledge map
Connecting knowledge across the PARC partnership

This interactive map prototype brings together a large part of the work produced within the PARC partnership, showing how research projects, partners, publications, chemicals and health effects are interconnected. It helps you to explore relationships across different areas of PARC's work and better understand the project's key messages.

The current version contains only limited information on chemicals. A future version will be integrated into the PARC website, allowing updates to projects and publications to be automatically reflected in the map

The information is visualised as a directed graph: a set of nodes connected by one-way links that represent relationships from one node to another, but not necessarily back. The map rearranges itself based on the node you select: the clicked node displays all its connections, while the others show only their strongest links, ensuring that each node appears only once, even when multiple interconnections exist.

The prototype is meant to illustrate the concept, explore its potential, and collect feedback for a future version that will be automatically update from eu- parc.eu. Clicking on the legend groups the information by topic, allowing you explore different thematic perspectives.

Known limitations

  • Largely based on data available in late 2025
  • The underlying data is not yet fully validated and includes synthetic examples
  • This version is made for the general meeting in Dublin and does not contains outside links

Planned improvements

  • Add links to publications via DOIs.
  • Expand information on chemicals and health effects.
  • Validate and enrich the content.
  • Improve map labels.
  • Integrate automatic updates from eu-parc.eu.

Future ideas

  • Link publication authors to partner organisations.
  • Connect the map to PARC thematic areas and tools.
  • Include information on policy relevance.
