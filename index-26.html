This prototype of an interactive map brings together a large part of the work produced within the PARC partnership, showing how research projects, partners, publications, chemicals and health effects are interconnected. It helps you to explore relationships across different areas of PARC's work and better understand the project’s key messages. It currently contains only limited information on chemicals and is a prototype that will later be integrated into the PARC website, so that updates to projects and publications there will automatically appear in the map.

The information is visualised as a directed graph: a set of nodes connected by one‑way edges that represent relationships from one node to another, but not necessarily back. The map is an interactive view that rearranges itself based on the node you select: the clicked node reveals all its connections, while the others show only their strongest links, ensuring that each node appears only once, even when multiple interconnections exist.

The prototype is meant to illustrate the concept, explore its potential, and collect feedback for a future version that will automatically updated from eu‑parc.eu. Clicking on the legend will group the information around the corresponding topics, helping you explore different thematic perspectives.

Known limitations:
Largely based on data available in late 2025
The underlying data is not yet fully validated and includes synthetic examples
This version is made for the general meeting in Dublin and does not contains outside links
